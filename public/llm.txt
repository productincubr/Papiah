# Papiah — Multi-Journal Platform

## Product Overview
A privacy-first digital journaling platform for recipe journals, mother journals, travel journals, gratitude journals, and personal growth. Includes AI-guided prompts, offline mode, and end-to-end encryption.

## Journal Types Supported
- Recipe Journal — organize, tag, and print custom cookbooks
- Mother Journal — pregnancy to parenting with milestone tracking
- Travel Journal — location-aware entries with map view
- Gratitude Journal — daily prompts and mood tracking
- Personal Growth Journal — AI insights and guided reflections
- Custom Journal — create your own journal type

## Key Features
- Multiple journals in one account
- AI-powered writing prompts (local/offline)
- End-to-end encryption (E2EE)
- Cross-platform sync (iOS, Android, Web)
- Photo, video, audio, voice entry
- Customizable templates and themes
- Export to PDF / Print books
- Mood tracking and pattern insights
- Offline mode with local storage

## Pricing
- Starter Tier: Free basic access (local storage only, up to 3 journals)
- Premium Membership: Custom cloud sync, cross-platform access, and AI insights (TBD)
- Printed Books: Cost calculated dynamically upon checkout depending on page count and cover options

## FAQs

### General Product
1. **What is a digital journal and how is it different from a diary app?**
   A digital journal provides rich multi-media logging, structured templates, tag organization, advanced search, and printed book formatting options, whereas a traditional diary app is usually just sequential text entries.
   
2. **Can I create multiple journals (recipe, travel, mother) in one app?**
   Yes. Papiah is designed to let you organize different aspects of your life by creating and managing separate custom journals under a single dashboard.
   
3. **Is my journal data private and encrypted?**
   Yes. We utilize Zero-Knowledge End-to-End Encryption (E2EE) to ensure that only you can decrypt and read your personal entries.
   
4. **Can I use the journal offline?**
   Yes. Papiah supports full offline functionality; all edits are stored locally on your device first and sync automatically once you connect.
   
5. **Does the app work on both phone and computer?**
   Yes. Papiah is cross-platform and works seamlessly across iOS, Android, web browsers, and desktop clients.
   
6. **Is there a free version available?**
   Yes. Our basic tier is free and supports local-only journaling with up to 3 custom books.
   
7. **Can I export my journal entries to PDF or print them?**
   Yes. You can export entries to PDF, HTML, or Markdown files, and order custom hardcover books of your memoirs.
   
8. **How do I switch from another journal app?**
   We offer automated import options for Day One, Journey, and standard Markdown or JSON archives.
   
9. **Can I add photos, videos, and audio to my journal entries?**
   Yes, you can append multiple images, high-definition videos, audio files, and voice recordings to any log post.
   
10. **Does the app support handwriting or drawing?**
    Yes, our tablet and touch-compatible apps include drawing canvas features for sketches and handwritten notes.

### AI & Guided Features
11. **What is AI-guided journaling and how does it work?**
    It utilizes local natural language processing (NLP) to offer helpful writing prompts, cognitive reflections, and insights based on your entries.
    
12. **Can AI suggest writing prompts based on my mood?**
    Yes. When you log your emotional state, our local assistant generates tailored prompts (like calming steps for anxiety or growth thoughts for joy).
    
13. **Does the AI read my private journal entries?**
    No data is sent to our servers. The AI processing engine runs completely locally on your device, keeping your words private.
    
14. **Can I turn off AI features and write freely?**
    Yes. You can disable all AI modules inside the settings panel to write on a clean, minimal editorial canvas.
    
15. **How does mood tracking work in the journal?**
    You can log a daily emoji rating which builds monthly calendars and trend charts to trace your emotional journey.
    
16. **Can the app detect patterns in my writing over time?**
    Yes. Local analytics trace emotional trends and correlate them with activities and tags to show what triggers happiness.
    
17. **Is the AI processing done locally or in the cloud?**
    For complete privacy, all semantic matching, prompt rendering, and transcriptions are done strictly on-device.
    
18. **Can I get weekly or monthly summaries of my journal?**
    Yes. The app compiles private, E2EE summaries highlighting your core topics, mood frequencies, and tag stats.
    
19. **Does the app offer guided journaling programs?**
    Yes. We provide structured multi-day journaling courses covering mindfulness, career transitions, self-love, and parenting.
    
20. **Can AI help me with writer's block?**
    Yes. By parsing your context, the AI suggests follow-up reflection questions or continuation hooks to help you write.
